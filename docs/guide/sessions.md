# Sessions

## It saves itself

Your project, images included, is kept in the browser (IndexedDB) as you work, so a reload brings it back.

## Take it with you

- **Export JSON** writes `sequentia-project.json`, and **Import JSON** brings one back
- **Nueva sesión** starts again from an empty project

## Undo

`Ctrl+Z` undoes and `Ctrl+Shift+Z` or `Ctrl+Y` redoes.

## Your sizes

The custom sizes you save with **Guardar preset** are kept in the browser, apart from the project.
