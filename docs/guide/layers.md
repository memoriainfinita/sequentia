# Layers and audio

The **Capas** panel, under **Composición**, adds what goes over the images and the music under them.

## Text

- **Título** and **Subtítulo (opcional)**
- **Fuente**: Inter, Playfair Display, Montserrat, Oswald or Lato; **Tamaño**, **Color** and **Opacidad**
- **Fondo** of the text: Ninguno, Píldora or Barra, with its own opacity
- **Animación**: none, Fade in or Slide up, and **Duración vis.** for how long it stays; empty means the whole slide
- **Posición X** and **Posición Y** place it on the image

This is the global text, shown on every slide. A slide can have its own or none: see «One slide different from the rest» in [Getting started](getting-started.md).

## Watermark, frame and vignette

- **Marca de agua** and **Marco** take a PNG each, drawn over every image; a frame is a PNG with a transparent middle
- **Viñeta** darkens the edges, with its intensity and colour

## Audio

Drop an MP3, WAV or OGG on the audio area, or click it. Then:

- **Trim In** and **Trim Out** set where the music starts and ends; **Volumen**, **Fade In** and **Fade Out** (with their length) shape it
- The player is only a preview: the export uses the Trim In and Out values
